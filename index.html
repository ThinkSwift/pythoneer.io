Pythoneer · a dig-and-build world with 38 games inside

One Tile at a Time

Pythoneer is one big world with thirty-eight small arcade games buried in it. Dig one up, beat it, and what you won comes back out with you — a deeper pickaxe, a lantern, a block to build with. Then make your own and bury it for a friend. This blog digs them up, one episode at a time.
