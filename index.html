pythoneer

little worlds,
made by hand.

drop a few tiles. nudge a number. press play — and something you made is suddenly alive, waiting for your friends to wander in.

free · iPhone & iPad
first —

a blank little world, all yours

no template gallery, no wizard. a quiet grid and a box of tiles. the first thing you place is the first rule of your world.

then —

break it, gently

change one number and watch everything tilt. gravity, speed, how high the crab jumps. this is the whole secret of coding — you just learned it by playing.

jump = 3  →  jump = 30  // oh no. oh yes.
finally —

let them in

share your world to Discover. a friend plays it, remixes it, and sends back something you don't quite recognize — your world, wearing their ideas.

under everything runs pythoneer script — a tiny language that thinks like python. build enough worlds, and one day you'll read the real thing like it was always yours.
