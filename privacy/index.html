Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026 · applies to Pythoneer and Spriteer

Our apps are built to work without accounts. We do not collect, sell, or share personal information for advertising, and we do not embed advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs.

What we collect

The apps never ask for your name, email, or any personal details, and we run no user accounts. To fix bugs and understand which features are used, the apps send a small number of anonymous usage events (for example “a night was won” or “a sprite was saved”) tagged with a random per-install identifier and basic app/OS version info. This identifier is not linked to you or your Apple ID, is not used for tracking or advertising, and matches what we declare in the App Store privacy label.

If you share analytics with app developers in your device’s settings, the apps also send the crash and performance reports Apple’s MetricKit gives them, with the same random identifier, so we can fix what broke.

Your saves in iCloud

Your world, your home and what you draw are saved on your device and, when you’re signed in to iCloud, in your own private iCloud database, so they carry on across your iPhone, iPad and Mac. That database belongs to your Apple ID — we can’t read it. On one device, Pythoneer and Spriteer share your home through an app group.

No AI

Pythoneer versions before 4.0 had an in-app ✨ helper that sent your prompt and game to our server and an AI model. Version 4.0 removed it; neither app sends anything to an AI model.

Feedback

If you write to us with Send feedback in Pythoneer’s settings, the message is kept for up to 90 days so we can read it and reply inside the app. It is tagged only with the same random identifier — no email or name.

No public sharing

Since version 3.1 the apps have no public feed, no usernames, and no way to publish to strangers. What you make stays yours — on your device and in your own iCloud — unless you export a file and send it yourself. If you shared worlds or skins before 3.1, you can remove them in the app (Pythoneer: Settings ▸ Paks I shared or Delete all data; Spriteer: Settings ▸ Delete my shared items), or contact us and we’ll remove them for you.

Third-party services

Apps distributed through the App Store rely on Apple for delivery, payments, and Apple’s own aggregated analytics.

Children

Our apps are for all ages and do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Each app has one purchase — The Portal in Pythoneer, Unlock All in Spriteer — handled by Apple, with Ask to Buy.

Changes

We may update this policy as the apps evolve; material changes appear here with a new effective date.
